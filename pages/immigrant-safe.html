Immigrant-Safe Campus Campaign

up-to-date Campaign Resolution
The Immigrant-Safe Campus Campaign is a pressure campaign started by the YDSA at BSU. Currently, we are asking for signatures on our petition which will be presented to the administration. Our demands are as follows:
  1. The university refuses to comply with DHS/ICE without a complete and relevant warrant
  2. The university issues a statement declaring our campus to be immigrant-safe
  3. The university recommits itself to protecting free speech for all students and faculty, and releases a statement affirming that commitment
  4. The university provides mandatory non-compliance training for all public facing employees
